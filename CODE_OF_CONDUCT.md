# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Ramrattan AI Editorial Studio is committed to a respectful, constructive, trustworthy, and welcoming environment.

We believe that trust is our most valuable feature.

Everyone participating in this project is expected to contribute in ways that strengthen both the software and the community.

Expected behaviour includes:

- Communicating professionally
- Giving constructive and actionable feedback
- Respecting differing experience levels
- Critiquing ideas rather than people
- Attributing contributions appropriately
- Disclosing uncertainty where appropriate
- Correcting errors openly
- Respecting evidence and source attribution
- Using AI assistance responsibly and transparently when appropriate
- Helping maintain a collaborative and inclusive environment

Unacceptable behaviour includes:

- Harassment
- Personal attacks
- Discrimination
- Plagiarism
- Deliberate misrepresentation
- Fabricating evidence or sources
- Publishing private or confidential information without permission
- Bad-faith participation
- Disruptive behaviour that prevents productive collaboration

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions that conflict with these standards.

Repeated or serious violations may result in temporary or permanent removal from the project.

## Reporting Concerns

Concerns should be raised privately with the repository owner through an appropriate GitHub channel.

Security vulnerabilities should be reported privately and should not be disclosed publicly until they have been acknowledged and addressed.

## Our Commitment

Every contributor deserves to be treated with professionalism and respect.

We value thoughtful discussion, evidence-based decisions, continuous learning, and the shared goal of building software that earns trust.
