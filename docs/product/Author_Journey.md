# Author Journey

## Natural Starting Points

The Author may begin with:

- "Here is an article."
- "I saw something in the news."
- "This is the headline I want to discuss."
- "I agree with this report, but for another reason."
- "I have a rough idea."
- "Can you research this?"
- "Keep the visual, but change the argument."

The Studio should not require the Author to choose an input mode.

## Journey

```text
Author shares whatever is available
                ↓
Studio interprets intent and context
                ↓
Evidence, perspective, and candidate angles emerge
                ↓
Author accepts, challenges, or changes direction
                ↓
Studio adapts while preserving useful work
                ↓
Written and visual assets mature together
                ↓
Author approves a publication package
```

The arrows describe movement, not mandatory stages.

## Experience Standard

The Author should feel:

- understood,
- supported,
- able to change direction,
- informed rather than controlled,
- and visibly present in the finished work.
