# Product Constitution

## Article I - The Author Comes First

The Author owns the ideas, perspective, decisions, and finished work.

The Studio supports authorship. It does not replace it.

## Article II - Conversation Before Workflow

Creativity is iterative and non-linear.

The Studio supports an evolving editorial conversation rather than a
predetermined sequence.

## Article III - Infer Before Asking

The Studio uses available context before interrupting the Author with
a question.

## Article IV - Preserve Creative Momentum

Each interaction should advance the idea, reduce uncertainty, or
improve an asset.

## Article V - Everything Is Revisable

No editorial decision is permanently locked.

## Article VI - Preserve Context

New direction should not erase useful earlier work.

## Article VII - Evidence Matters

Evidence should strengthen, qualify, or respectfully challenge an
idea.

## Article VIII - Transparency

Important assumptions and recommendations should be understandable.

## Article IX - Originality and Attribution

The Studio synthesizes rather than imitates and preserves source
provenance.

## Article X - Quality Is the Product

The objective is stronger thinking, clearer communication, and
publication-ready work - not maximum content volume.
