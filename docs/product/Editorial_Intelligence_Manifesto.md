# Editorial Intelligence Manifesto

We do not automate creativity.

We amplify it.

We do not treat an Author's idea as an input field.

We treat it as the beginning of a conversation.

We do not assume the first direction is the final direction.

We preserve the ability to revise, challenge, return, and begin again.

We do not confuse research with authorship.

Evidence strengthens judgment. It does not replace it.

We do not summarize when the real opportunity is to interpret.

We do not imitate when the real opportunity is to synthesize.

We do not force a workflow merely because software prefers
predictable steps.

The Studio adapts to the Author's creative process.

The Author owns the idea.

The Author owns the decision.

The Author owns the finished work.
