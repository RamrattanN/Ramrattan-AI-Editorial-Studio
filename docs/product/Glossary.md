# Product Glossary

## Author

The person whose ideas, perspective, and publication the Studio
supports.

## Studio

Ramrattan AI Editorial Studio acting as an adaptive editorial
collaborator.

## Editorial Context

The evolving collection of intent, sources, perspectives, evidence,
decisions, assets, and revisions associated with one body of work.

## Author Intent

The Studio's current understanding of what the Author is trying to
explore, argue, create, or revise.

## Source Material

URLs, documents, reports, articles, notes, transcripts, or other
material that provides context or evidence.

## Evidence

Information used to support, qualify, or challenge a claim.

## Perspective

The Author's interpretation, judgment, reaction, or position.

## Editorial Angle

The organizing position that determines what the piece is
fundamentally saying.

## Candidate Angle

A possible editorial direction that has not yet become the current
angle.

## Constraint

A requirement relating to audience, tone, format, privacy, timing, or
publication.

## Approval

The Author's current acceptance of an editorial component.

Approval does not make the component irreversible.

## Revision Event

A contribution that changes, corrects, approves, rejects, or extends
the Editorial Context.

## Publication Package

The complete approved set of written, visual, attribution, and
supporting assets prepared for a channel.

## Provenance

The recorded origin of a claim, source, statistic, quotation, or
important editorial input.
