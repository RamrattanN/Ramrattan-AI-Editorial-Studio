# Product Requirements Document - Version 1.0

## Product

Ramrattan AI Editorial Studio

## North Star

> Ramrattan AI Editorial Studio is an adaptive editorial partner
> that helps Authors transform evolving ideas, evidence, expertise,
> and perspective into publication-ready thought leadership.

> The Studio adapts to the Author's creative process - never the
> other way around.

## Purpose

The Studio helps Authors discover, strengthen, refine, and
communicate ideas.

It is not limited to summarizing source material or converting a
URL into a predetermined template.

## Supported Starting Points

An Author may begin with:

- one or more URLs,
- a headline,
- a topic,
- something seen in the news,
- a personal observation,
- a developed perspective,
- rough notes,
- a draft,
- or any useful combination.

The Author is not required to classify the input before the Studio
begins interpreting it.

## Core Behaviour

The Studio must:

1. Interpret the Author's likely intent from available context.
2. Distinguish evidence from perspective.
3. Infer before asking unnecessary questions.
4. Research externally when evidence or current information is needed.
5. Preserve the Author's language, intent, and approved work.
6. Permit any editorial decision to be revisited.
7. Recalculate only assets affected by a change.
8. Maintain provenance for claims and source material.
9. Produce coherent written and visual publication assets.

## Adaptive Editorial Conversation

Creativity does not follow a fixed sequence.

The Author may:

- introduce another source after drafting begins,
- change the central argument,
- remove an earlier perspective,
- change the output format,
- retain a visual while rewriting the article,
- retain the argument while replacing the visual,
- or return to an earlier editorial direction.

## Editorial Context

The Studio maintains an evolving Editorial Context containing,
where available:

- Author intent,
- source material,
- observations,
- Author perspectives,
- supporting notes,
- constraints,
- candidate angles,
- current editorial angle,
- supporting evidence,
- unresolved questions,
- visual concepts,
- generated assets,
- approvals,
- revisions,
- and publication readiness.

Each component may be missing, inferred, proposed, approved,
revised, superseded, rejected, or removed.

## Questions and Options

The Studio asks a question only when ambiguity materially blocks
progress or creates a meaningful risk of producing the wrong result.

Numbered or clickable options should be used only when:

- a genuine editorial decision exists,
- alternatives are meaningfully distinct,
- and selecting an option reduces effort.

Options must not become a compulsory intake workflow.

## Evidence and Research

Evidence should support, qualify, or respectfully challenge an idea.

Evidence must not silently replace the Author's perspective.

## Initial Outputs

The first production module supports:

- LinkedIn articles,
- seven-slide LinkedIn carousels,
- LinkedIn hero graphics,
- headlines,
- hooks,
- insights,
- practical takeaways,
- source attribution,
- calls to action,
- hashtags,
- and LinkedIn descriptions.

LinkedIn is the initial channel, not the permanent boundary of the
product.

## Non-Functional Requirements

The product should be:

- understandable,
- reversible,
- maintainable,
- testable,
- source-aware,
- transparent about assumptions,
- resilient to changing direction,
- and independent of one AI provider.

## Success Measures

Success is measured by whether the Studio helps Authors:

- articulate stronger ideas,
- preserve authentic perspective,
- develop defensible claims,
- revise without losing momentum,
- create coherent written and visual work,
- and remain in control of the finished publication.

Content volume is not a primary success measure.

## Existing Workflow Prototype

The current linear workflow package remains useful as an engineering
experiment.

It is not the final product interaction model.
