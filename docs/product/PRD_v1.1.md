# Product Requirements Document - Version 1.1

## Product

Ramrattan AI Editorial Studio

## Status

Current product baseline

## Supersedes

PRD v1.0 as the active product definition.

PRD v1.0 remains preserved as historical context.

## North Star

> Ramrattan AI Editorial Studio is an adaptive editorial operating
> system that helps Authors transform evolving ideas, evidence,
> expertise, and perspective into publication-ready professional
> articles.

> The Studio adapts to the Author's creative process - never the
> other way around.

## Product Focus

The current product is focused on professional articles.

Each completed article includes a supporting 720 × 425 Hero Visual
designed for LinkedIn article presentation.

Carousels and unrelated content formats are outside the active
product scope.

## Primary Product Outcome

A publication package containing:

- Hero Visual - 720 × 425
- Headline
- Publication-ready article
- Source attribution
- Practical takeaway
- Call to action
- Hashtags
- LinkedIn description
- Publication metadata

## Time-to-Value Requirement

When the Author provides sufficient material, the Studio should aim
to produce the first publication-ready article package within
approximately 10 minutes.

The Author remains responsible for final review and publication.

## Supported Starting Points

The Author may begin with:

- URLs
- Copied source material
- Headlines
- Topics
- News recollections
- Observations
- Perspectives
- Notes
- Drafts
- Corrections
- Revisions
- Any useful combination

The Author is not required to select an input category.

## Discernment Requirement

The Studio should infer:

- what the Author is discussing,
- what the Author appears to believe,
- what evidence is already present,
- what evidence may be required,
- what remains ambiguous,
- and what action is most useful next.

Questions should be reserved for ambiguity that materially blocks
quality or correctness.

## Input Quality Requirement

The Studio must evaluate whether the available material can support
a distinctive and defensible article.

If it cannot, the Studio must not generate generic filler.

It should:

- explain why the input is insufficient,
- identify what is missing,
- provide an example of a stronger input,
- and recommend the smallest next action.

## Originality Requirement

Output must synthesize rather than imitate.

Reference content may inform:

- facts,
- evidence,
- sentiment,
- and context.

It must not determine distinctive wording, structure, framing, or
expression.

## Adaptive Editorial Context

The Editorial Context is the active representation of an article
project.

It may include:

- Author intent
- Inputs
- Source material
- Author perspectives
- Evidence
- Provenance
- Candidate angles
- Current thesis
- Article structure
- Hero Visual concepts
- Draft assets
- Decisions
- Revision events
- Publication readiness

The context must support non-linear revision.

## Article Engine

The Article Engine consumes and updates the Editorial Context.

It is responsible for:

- developing the thesis,
- constructing the argument,
- structuring the article,
- drafting and revising copy,
- maintaining originality,
- incorporating evidence,
- and assembling the publication package.

## Hero Visual

The Hero Visual is part of the article publication package.

Requirements:

- 720 × 425
- Landscape orientation
- Visually communicates the article thesis
- Brand-neutral
- No logos or watermarks
- No recognizable faces by default
- Editable hero copy
- Strong mobile readability
- Photorealistic unless another treatment is explicitly approved

## Author Library

The Author Library stores paused and completed article projects.

Initial local-first requirements:

- Save
- Load
- List
- Review
- Resume
- Revise
- Duplicate
- Archive
- Restore
- Export
- Delete

Author work is private by default.

## Initial Success Measures

- Time to first publication-ready package
- Author confidence to publish
- Originality
- Evidence quality
- Perspective preservation
- Revision continuity
- Hero Visual and article alignment
- Successful handling of insufficient starting material

## Non-Goals

The current product will not attempt to:

- Generate carousels
- Become a general social-content suite
- Force structured intake
- Replace Author judgment
- Hide assumptions
- Directly publish without approval
- Provide cloud collaboration during the local-first phase
