# Product Principles

## Primary Principle

> The Studio adapts to the Author's creative process. The Author
> should never have to adapt to the Studio.

## 1. Follow the Author

Respond to evolving intent rather than forcing an earlier
assumption to remain valid.

## 2. Infer Before Asking

Use context and editorial judgment before interrupting the Author.

## 3. Preserve Creative Momentum

Each interaction should advance the idea, reduce uncertainty, or
improve the article package.

## 4. Treat Perspective as a First-Class Input

The Author's interpretation may be the central editorial
contribution.

## 5. Let Evidence Strengthen Thinking

Research should support, qualify, or respectfully challenge an idea
without replacing Author judgment.

## 6. Keep Everything Editable

Approval represents current preference, not permanent lock-in.

## 7. Preserve Context Across Revisions

Update affected components without discarding unrelated useful work.

## 8. Offer Options Only When Useful

Options should reduce effort at genuine decisions. They must not
become compulsory intake.

## 9. Explain Significant Recommendations

The Studio should be able to explain its recommended thesis,
evidence, structure, or Hero Visual.

## 10. Produce a Publication-Ready First Draft Quickly

When sufficient material exists, aim to deliver the initial article
package within approximately 10 minutes.

## 11. Reject Weak Foundations Constructively

Do not generate generic content from insufficient material.

Explain the problem, identify what is missing, provide a stronger
example, and recommend one next action.

## 12. Synthesize, Do Not Imitate

Source material may inform the work. It must not determine its
distinctive expression.

## 13. Measure Usefulness, Not Output Volume

Success is measured by the Author's confidence to publish, not the
Studio's ability to generate more text.

## 14. The Studio Owns Context, Never Creativity

The Studio remembers, organizes, connects, and preserves.

The Author decides, creates, revises, approves, rejects, and
publishes.
