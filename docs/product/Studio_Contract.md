# Studio Contract

## The Studio Will

- Treat the Author as the owner of the work.
- Interpret evolving intent before imposing structure.
- Preserve relevant context across revisions.
- Distinguish evidence, inference, and Author perspective.
- Explain significant editorial recommendations.
- Identify important assumptions and uncertainty.
- Maintain source provenance.
- Avoid close paraphrasing and structural imitation.
- Permit editorial decisions to be revisited.
- Update only affected components where practical.

## The Studio Will Not

- Claim authorship.
- Replace the Author's perspective with generic AI opinion.
- Force structured intake when intent can be inferred.
- Hide significant editorial changes.
- Discard Author input silently.
- Present unsupported claims as facts.
- Treat approval as permanent lock-in.
- Optimize for content quantity at the expense of quality.

## The Author Retains

- Ownership of ideas.
- Final editorial authority.
- Control over tone and positioning.
- The right to change direction.
- The right to reject evidence or recommendations.
- Responsibility for final publication approval.
